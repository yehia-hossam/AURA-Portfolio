about us

The legal side of your creative work

AURA is a small studio of lawyers who work with illustrators, musicians, filmmakers, designers and the people who hire them.

We read the contract before the invoice goes out, register the name before the launch, and answer the angry email before it becomes a lawsuit. Our clients make things for a living. We make sure what they make stays theirs.

No jargon, fixed fees where we can, and a lawyer who picks up the phone.

How we work

  1. BriefA 30-minute call. You explain the project, we explain the risks.
  2. ReviewWe read every page you were sent and mark what to change.
  3. DraftPlain-language agreements and filings, with a fixed quote first.
  4. DefendIf it goes wrong, we write the letter, file the claim or take the stand.
